About

Hi, I’m Chelsea!

  • Daughter to two of the best parents in the world (I’m biased, but it’s still true).
  • Youngest of 3 siblings.
  • Professional cool aunt since 1997 (promoted to great-aunt in 2025).
  • Music-lover.
  • Writer at heart.
  • Daily photographer.

And a second-generation Amway Independent Business Owner. And proud of it!

Follow on Instagram for more everyday moments (opens a new tab)

My parents taught me, mostly by living it, that mindset is everything and that proximity is powerful: the people around you shape what you think is possible. I’ve watched lives change when people get access to the right opportunities, people and products, and I always knew I’d want to be a part of that.

I’ve also lived with disabilities my whole life and been through more surgeries and hospital stays than I’d like to count, so I know what hard feels like. If you’re on the struggle bus somewhere, I’ve been there too. But we don’t have to build our lives there. We can do better. If you believe that (or want to believe it!), then you’re one of my people & I’m so excited to meet you!

How I operate

You’re my friend,
not an order number.

I’d rather earn your trust over time than win one sale. Here’s what you can count on.

My promise

You’ll always know who to ask, you’ll never be pressured, and you’ll always get a real answer from a real person.

  • I listen first.

    Before I suggest anything, I ask what’s going on and what you’d like to be different.

  • I start small.

    A few well-chosen things, or samples, so you can see what fits before you commit.

  • I tell you the truth.

    If something I carry isn’t right for you, I’ll say so.

  • I remember.

    I keep track of what you’re using, so I can reach out before you run out, not after.

  • I adjust.

    Life changes. When yours does, I’ll help you find what fits now.

Questions & answers

Who is Uncommonly Well?

Uncommonly Well is the business of Chelsea Herschelman, an Amway Independent Business Owner. The products are Amway products. The service, the follow-up and the care are me.

Do I need a membership to make a purchase?

Nope! Anyone can order with a free Customer Profile. If you order regularly, though, the new Amway Membership (launching mid-November) is worth a look: for $20 a month, it unlocks up to $100 in rewards and discounts, including $40 off orders of $150 or more and free priority shipping.

How do samples work?

Tell me what you’re looking for and I’ll put together a few that fit, while supplies last. No commitments.

How do I place an order?

Visit my shop on Amway (opens Amway), create a free Customer Profile & add to cart!

Can I return something I don’t love?

Please do! I only want you to keep things you absolutely love, and Amway guarantees your satisfaction. Most products can be returned within 180 days of purchase for an exchange or a refund of the price and tax. Just let me know and we’ll take care of it.

Will I get spammed?

Never! Every message comes from me, written just for you. Want to hear from me more, less, or not at all? Just say the word.

Why order through you?

Amway has been making high-quality everyday products since 1959—and they’re good at it. They take care of the products; I take care of the person. I’m here to listen, help you find what fits, remember what you use & stay one message away when you have a question.

How do I reach you?

Just . I read & respond to every one myself.

Not sure where to start? That’s what I’m here for.
