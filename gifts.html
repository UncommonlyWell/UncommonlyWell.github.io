Gifts

Gifts, done uncommonly well.

Pick a ready-made bundle, or tell me about the person and I’ll put one together. I’ll wrap it and get it where it needs to go.

Have someone tricky to shop for?

Every gift is built to order, so the box or basket might look a little different from the photo. I promise it’ll be just as cute.

    Something custom

    Tell me who it’s for, and I’ll build it.

    What they love, the occasion, your budget, when it needs to be there. I’ll choose a few things that fit them and wrap it all up.

    How gifting works

    1. 1

      Tell me who it’s for.

      Choose a bundle, or tell me a little about them: what they love, the occasion and your budget.

    2. 2

      I put it together.

      Ready-made or built from scratch, I gather everything and wrap it by hand. The packaging may change with what’s on hand, but never the care.

    3. 3

      It gets where it’s going.

      Shipped to them, shipped to you, or hand-delivered if they’re local. You just get the thank-you.

    Not sure what they’d like? That’s what I’m here for.
