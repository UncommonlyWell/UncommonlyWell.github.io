Valdura · the health app

Your bloodwork, in plain language

Valdura is a health app from the same company family as Avellum. It reads your bloodwork and lab results, tracks them over time and answers questions about them.

  • An assistant that knows your numbers. Reads your labs, wearables and logs, and remembers your goals.
  • Your bloodwork in plain language. Every marker explained, and how each one changes over time.
  • A plan built from your data. One plan for eating, training and what to take, plus a seven-day meal plan.
  • A few taps a day. A daily check-in, streaks and daily puzzles.
  • Meals and wearables in one place. Snap a photo for calories and macros; connect Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • Private by design. Never sold. Export or delete anytime.
